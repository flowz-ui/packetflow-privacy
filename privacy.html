PacketFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

PacketFlow is published by Avniye Kaçak. This policy explains what information PacketFlow handles.

1. Information PacketFlow handles

PacketFlow does not send user data to our servers, does not require an account, and does not track users. To do its job, PacketFlow stores the following only on your device:

  • Settings: theme, split-tunnel mode, and the list of selected apps.
  • Last connection status and error logs: the reason for the last error and crash information. Stored only on the device.
  • Live traffic counters: connection speed and total data transferred. Held in memory only while PacketFlow is open and never saved.

2. Network traffic

While connected, the network traffic of apps on your device is routed through a local tunnel inside the device. This traffic goes directly from your device to the destinations your apps connect to. It is not sent to PacketFlow's servers or to any other third party. PacketFlow does not record or store this traffic, and does not use it for advertising or analytics.

3. Third parties

PacketFlow does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. PacketFlow contains no SDK for these purposes.

4. Permissions

  • VPN service (VpnService): Needed to route the network traffic of apps on your device through the local tunnel. You can remove this permission at any time from Android's system VPN settings.
  • Internet and network state: To run the tunnel and check whether a connection is available.
  • Notifications: To show your connection status.
  • Installed app list: So you can choose apps in the split-tunnel settings. This list is not sent off your device.

5. Storage and deletion

All data PacketFlow saves stays on your device. Uninstalling PacketFlow deletes this data. You can also delete it by clearing PacketFlow's data in Android settings.

6. Users in Turkey (KVKK)

Personal data processed in Turkey is covered by Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK). The data controller is Avniye Kaçak. Because PacketFlow does not collect personal data in the way described above, there is little data to access or correct. To exercise your rights under KVKK, contact us at flowent.contact@gmail.com.

7. Children

PacketFlow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page and the "Last updated" date is changed.

9. Contact

For questions: flowent.contact@gmail.com
