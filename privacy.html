PacketFlow Privacy Policy

Kinetic Studio (owner and developer: Avniye Kaçak, individual developer account) · Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Contact: flowent.contact@gmail.com

Summary

PacketFlow runs a local VPN tunnel on your phone. The developer does not run a server that your traffic passes through, and the developer does not collect, store or sell your browsing data, IP addresses or personal information.

How the VPN works

PacketFlow uses Android's VpnService API to route the traffic of the apps you choose through a tunnel that runs on your device. Your traffic then goes directly from your phone to the sites and services you use. It does not pass through any PacketFlow server. We cannot see which sites you visit or what you send or receive.

Information that leaves your device

  • DNS lookups. To resolve site names, PacketFlow sends encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS requests to public resolvers: Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and Google Public DNS (8.8.8.8). These providers receive the domain names that your apps look up and your IP address, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive this data.
  • Speed test. Only when you tap the speed test, PacketFlow connects to speed.cloudflare.com to measure ping, download and upload. Cloudflare receives your IP address and the test traffic under its own privacy policy.
  • Feedback. If you choose "Send feedback", your email app opens with a message you can edit. Optional device information (app version, Android version, device model, language) is attached only if you leave that option on. Nothing is sent unless you send the email.

Information stored on your device

PacketFlow stores your settings (theme, language, font) and your split-tunnel app list on your device. The app list and error notes are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. App backup is turned off. PacketFlow does not write packet contents, IP addresses or visited sites to logs.

Permissions

  • VPN (BIND_VPN_SERVICE / VpnService): needed to create the tunnel. It is used only for this purpose.
  • Internet and network state: needed to connect and to show connection status.
  • Foreground service: keeps the tunnel running while the app is in the background, with a visible notification.
  • Notifications: shows the connection status.

PacketFlow does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS or files.

Sharing, selling and advertising

The developer does not sell or share personal data. PacketFlow contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not inject, replace or redirect ads or traffic.

Children

PacketFlow is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time from the app or the notification, choose which apps use the tunnel, and uninstall PacketFlow to remove all data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
